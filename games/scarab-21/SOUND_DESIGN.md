# Scarab 21 audio

The placement cue is a 130 ms softened card-slide rustle adapted from
Kenney Vleugels's [Casino Audio 1.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio)
(`card-slide-2.ogg`, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).
Its gentle onset and fading tail avoid the prior sharp impact.

Column sweep and board fan use the pack's `card-shove-4.ogg` and
`card-fan-1.ogg` respectively, trimmed and converted to mono.
The board fan includes a short chime. Game over has its own closing cue.

**Sound is enabled by default**; the Mute sound button is opt-in,
and the browser audio context resumes after a user gesture.

Listen through 30–50 placements on laptop speakers and headphones.
Compare the replacement against the old cue and silence at matched volume,
and check that clears and game-over remain distinct during fast play.
