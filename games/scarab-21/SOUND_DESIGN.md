# Scarab 21 audio

The card slide, column sweep and board fan use Kenney Vleugels's
[Casino Audio 1.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) recordings
([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).
They use `card-slide-2.ogg`, `card-shove-4.ogg` and `card-fan-1.ogg`
respectively, trimmed to the action and converted to mono. The board fan
includes a short chime. Game over has its own closing cue. Sound is on by
default, with a Mute sound button.

Listen through 30–50 placements on laptop speakers and headphones.
Compare the slide at matched listening level with the previous cue and silence.
Check that the clears and the blocked draw stay distinct during fast play.
