HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to 2048! Your goal is to combine numbers until you reach the 2048 tile.

1. Move Your Tiles
Use your arrow keys or WASD to move tiles around.

2. Combine Numbers
Match two tiles with the same number to combine them.

3. Reach 2048
Keep combining numbers until you create the 2048 tile!

4. Don't Fill the Board
If there are no empty spaces or matching neighbours, the game is over.
