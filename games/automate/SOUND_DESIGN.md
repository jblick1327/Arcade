# Automate audio

The original piece and capture samples cover ordinary moves and checks.
Draft placement reuses the piece sample at lower gain. Castling uses two taps.
Promotion, checkmate and draws add short rendered chimes over the move.
Sound is on by default and starts after interaction if the browser requires it.
The Mute sound button uses the existing mix level when sound is on.

Listen at 100 ms and 600 ms think time. Check the move/capture balance,
capture into checkmate, and the difference between a win and a draw.
